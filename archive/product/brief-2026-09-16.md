# Product brief — in-person loyalty for small stores

## Vision

Small in-person businesses run a digital “buy X, get 1 free” card that customers actually keep: no download, signup via a QR at the till, email + phone on file, an Apple/Google Wallet pass for fast scans, and a stamp loop fast enough for a busy counter. Owners go live in minutes; staff stamp in seconds. First market is **Montevideo, Uruguay** (Spanish); rest of Uruguay and other Spanish-speaking countries after pilots.

## Who

Primary user is the owner (or day manager) of a single-location, in-person business that can run a classic stamp card — café, salon/barber, boutique, and any similar shop where “buy X, get 1 free” makes sense — who wants more repeat visits without hiring a marketer or learning a CRM.

The end customer is a walk-in who will not install another app but will scan a QR once at the register and keep a Wallet pass (or give their phone/email at the till).

## Problem

Paper punch cards get lost; full loyalty apps feel heavy for a local shop. Owners need a fast check-in at the till and a clear reason to return. Why now: phones already hold Wallet passes, so a no-app stamp card is credible without building native apps.

## What it is

The smallest product that proves the vision:

- **Default reward model:** stamp card — buy 9, get 1 free by default; merchant can change X (buy X, get 1 free). Not a points engine in v1.
- Merchant creates a program in under ~10 minutes: stamp count X, what counts as a stamp, what the free reward is, and whether the freebie can be redeemed alone or only with another purchase. Starter templates for café / salon / boutique (and a generic “buy X get 1 free”) prefills — same product for all, not separate products per vertical.
- **Signup at the till:** every shop has a signup QR at the cash register. Customer scans → **name + email + phone/WhatsApp number** → Add to Apple/Google Wallet. No username in v1.
- **Stamp on later visits:** staff scans the customer’s **Wallet QR**, or the customer gives their **phone number or email** and staff looks it up.
- Each qualifying visit adds one stamp. After X paid stamps, a following visit can unlock the free item; staff redeems at the till; card resets for the next cycle. Freebie-only visit vs must-buy-something is a **merchant setting**.
- Simple merchant view: visits, stamps issued, freebies redeemed.

UI and copy for v1: Spanish-first, tuned for **Montevideo, Uruguay** as the first pilot city.

Monetization direction: subscription per location around **USD 9–14 / month** (Martin working range; not yet validated with buyers).

## What it is not (v1)

- Not a customer-facing native app.
- Not usernames / short member codes (deferred; email + phone only for now).
- Not automated WhatsApp/SMS nudges (“close to freebie” / “gone cold”) — those are **v2**.
- Not a full email marketing / CRM suite.
- Not a points / tier engine (stamps only; points can come later).
- Not online/e-commerce loyalty as the primary use case.
- Not multi-location enterprise.
- Not a deep POS integration or cash-machine software embed — v1 is a signup QR **at** the register plus a merchant phone/tablet beside the till to scan Wallet or look up phone/email.

## Success

Observable signals that the idea is working (targets to be set after first pilots, not invented here):

- A store completes setup and runs the live stamp loop the same day.
- Staff complete enroll + stamp without training friction.
- A meaningful share of new members add the Wallet pass (phone/email lookup covers the rest).
- Return visits and freebie redemptions show up in the dashboard within the first weeks of a pilot.
- Owners renew because they see people coming back — not because of feature count.
- Pilots run in **Montevideo** with Spanish product copy.

## Risks and open questions

**Risks:** crowded category; till friction if Wallet scan or phone/email lookup is slow; staff forget to stamp; Apple/Google pass certificates and update reliability; horizontal “anyone with buy X get 1 free” makes sales messaging broad — starter templates must carry the specificity; without v1 messaging, return lift depends on the Wallet habit and a desirable freebie; Uruguay/Montevideo Wallet, WhatsApp, and consent norms need a light check before build.

**Open questions:**

1. v2 messaging channel when nudges land — WhatsApp Business vs SMS (Uruguay rules)?
2. Prefer phone or email as the primary verbal lookup at the till when there is no Wallet pass? (Both collected; one should be the default search.)

## Recommendation

Harden and send to PRD. Loop and scope are locked: till signup QR, email + phone identity, Wallet scan or phone/email lookup, buy-X-get-1-free stamps, merchant-controlled freebie rules, **Montevideo first**, no username and no automated messaging in v1, horizontal ICP with starter templates.
