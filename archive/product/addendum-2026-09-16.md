## Decisions from shaping (2026-09-16)

- No customer app; Wallet pass + till signup QR + stamp via Wallet QR **or** phone/email lookup.
- Signup at cash register QR: **name + email + phone/WhatsApp**. No username in v1.
- **ICP:** horizontal — café, salon, boutique, and anyone who can run buy X get 1 free. Starter templates for setup speed.
- **Geography:** **Montevideo, Uruguay first**; Spanish-first; rest of Uruguay and other Spanish-speaking countries later.
- Default reward: buy 9 get 1 free; X configurable. Points deferred.
- **Freebie-only visit:** merchant setting.
- Automated WhatsApp/SMS nudges → **v2**.
- No deep POS embed in v1; QR at register + merchant device beside till.
- **Username / member short code → deferred** (Martin: “no username yet then. just mail + number”).

- **Pricing working range (2026-09-16):** ~**USD 9–14 / month** per location (below many public comps such as Fielza $29+; intentional entry price — validate willingness-to-pay in Montevideo pilots).
